DATER COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Effective Date: 7 August 2026
Last Updated: 7 August 2026

Welcome to Dater.

We believe great dating starts with trust.

Dater is built for eligible adults (18 years of age or older, or the minimum age required to use online dating services in their country, whichever is higher) seeking genuine friendships, meaningful relationships, and authentic connections. Every member plays an important role in creating a safe, respectful, and welcoming community.

These Community Guidelines explain the standards we expect from everyone using Dater. They are designed to help protect our community, promote positive interactions, and reduce harmful behaviour.

These Community Guidelines supplement our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, but do not replace them. If there is any inconsistency between these Community Guidelines and the Terms & Conditions, the Terms & Conditions shall prevail.

By using Dater, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines and help us build a community based on respect, honesty, and safety.

 
1. OUR COMMUNITY VALUES

Everything we do at Dater is guided by a few simple principles.

Be Authentic

Use your real identity and represent yourself honestly. Genuine connections begin with genuine people.

Be Respectful

Treat every member with kindness, courtesy, and respect, even when opinions or interests differ.

Stay Safe

Help us create a community where people feel comfortable connecting without fear of scams, harassment, abuse, or exploitation.

Respect Privacy

Protect your own privacy and respect the privacy of others.

Build Meaningful Connections

Dater is designed for people looking to form genuine friendships and meaningful personal or romantic relationships. Help us keep the platform focused on that purpose.

 
2. WHO CAN USE DATER

Dater is intended exclusively for eligible adults (18 years of age or older, or the minimum age required to use online dating services in their country, whichever is higher).

You may use Dater only if you:

• are at least 18 years old, or meet the minimum age required to use online dating services in your country, whichever is higher;
• are legally permitted to use online dating services under applicable law;
• provide truthful information;
• comply with these Community Guidelines and our Terms & Conditions; and
• use the platform for lawful and genuine purposes.
 
We do not permit individuals below the age of 18, or below the minimum age required to use online dating services in their country, whichever is higher, to create accounts or use the Services.

If we believe that an account belongs to an individual below the age of 18, or below the minimum age required to use online dating services in their country, whichever is higher, we may suspend or permanently remove the account and take any additional action required or permitted by applicable law.

 
3. BE YOURSELF

Authenticity is one of the most important parts of building trust.

Always represent yourself honestly.

You should:

• use your real identity
• provide accurate profile information
• use recent photographs of yourself
• be honest about your age and other profile details
• interact with others in good faith; and
• keep your profile information reasonably up to date.
 
You must not:

• impersonate another person
• create fake profiles
• pretend to represent another individual or organisation
• use someone else’s photographs
• mislead others about your identity
• intentionally provide false or materially misleading information; or
• create multiple personal accounts without our permission.
 
Fake accounts reduce trust across the community and may be removed without notice.

 
4. USE REAL PROFILE PHOTOS

Your profile photos should clearly represent you.

Every profile photo uploaded to Dater must contain a clearly visible human face and should accurately reflect your appearance.

To help maintain an authentic community, Dater may use automated technologies, including Amazon Web Services (AWS) Rekognition, together with human review where appropriate, to assist in detecting profile photos that do not meet our authenticity standards.

The following are not permitted as profile photos:

• photographs of another person
• celebrity photographs
• influencer photographs
• AI-generated or manipulated images intended to mislead others
• cartoons or avatars used in place of your own face
• anime or fictional characters
• pets or animals without you
• cars, motorcycles, or other vehicles without you
• landscapes or scenery without you
• logos, brand images, or promotional graphics
• screenshots
• memes
• text-only images
• images containing QR codes or URLs intended to direct users to external websites or services; or
• any image that does not contain a clearly visible human face.
 
The use of ordinary photo-editing tools, filters, or AI-assisted editing is not prohibited solely because AI or automated technology was used, provided that the resulting content does not materially misrepresent your identity or appearance or otherwise violate these Community Guidelines.

If your profile photos do not comply with these Guidelines, Dater may ask you to replace them, remove them, restrict certain features, or take other appropriate moderation action.

A verified profile badge indicates only that Dater has completed the applicable verification process. It is not a guarantee of a person’s identity, honesty, intentions, character, conduct, or safety.

 
5. TREAT EVERYONE WITH RESPECT

Every person on Dater deserves to be treated with kindness, dignity, and respect.

Healthy relationships begin with respectful communication, even when conversations do not lead to a friendship or romantic connection.

We encourage you to:

• be polite and considerate
• respect personal boundaries
• communicate honestly
• accept rejection gracefully
• respect different opinions and lifestyles; and
• help create a welcoming community.
 
The following behaviour is not permitted:

• harassment
• bullying
• intimidation
• stalking
• threats
• abusive or threatening language
• repeated unwanted contact
• unwanted sexual advances
• discrimination
• hate speech
• encouraging violence
• humiliating or degrading another person
• encouraging self-harm; or
• any behaviour that makes another member feel unsafe.
 
Remember that everyone has the right to stop a conversation or decline a friend request. Respect their decision and move on.

6. HATE SPEECH & DISCRIMINATION

Dater is for people from different backgrounds, cultures, identities, and life experiences. Everyone deserves to feel welcome and respected.

We do not tolerate hate speech, discrimination, or attacks against people based on characteristics such as:

• race or ethnicity
• nationality or national origin
• caste
• religion or belief
• gender or gender identity
• sexual orientation
• disability
• age; or
• other protected characteristics under applicable law.
 
You must not:

• promote hatred against a group or individual
• use slurs or demeaning language targeting a protected characteristic
• encourage discrimination, exclusion, or violence
• threaten or celebrate violence against people because of their identity
• dehumanise or degrade people based on protected characteristics; or
• create content intended to intimidate or make members of a protected group feel unsafe.
 
Respectful discussion about different cultures, beliefs, identities, and experiences is allowed. However, disagreement does not justify harassment, hatred, threats, or discrimination.

Content that violates these standards may be removed, and serious or repeated violations may result in suspension or permanent removal from Dater.

 
7. KEEP CONVERSATIONS SAFE

Dater is designed to help people build genuine connections.

Please communicate responsibly and respectfully.

When chatting with other members:

• be honest about your intentions
• respect personal boundaries
• avoid pressuring someone to share personal information
• report suspicious behaviour; and
• think carefully before sharing sensitive information.
 
Never ask another member to provide passwords, OTPs, verification codes, payment credentials, or other information that could be used to access or compromise an account or financial account.

For your safety, avoid sharing information such as:

• your bank account details
• debit or credit card information
• OTPs or verification codes
• passwords
• financial information
• government-issued identity documents; or
• other sensitive personal information.
 
You should also avoid sharing your phone number, email address, or social media accounts until you know and trust the other person.

If someone makes you feel uncomfortable, you can stop communicating, block the user, and report the conversation.

 
8. DATING, NOT SCAMMING

Dater exists to help people build genuine friendships and meaningful relationships — not to deceive or exploit others.

Scams and financial exploitation are strictly prohibited.

Never:

• ask another member for money
• request loans
• request UPI transfers
• request gift cards
• request cryptocurrency
• promote investment opportunities
• offer guaranteed investment returns
• ask someone to pay your bills
• invent emergencies to obtain money
• impersonate military personnel, doctors, celebrities, government officials, or other trusted individuals for deceptive purposes
• recruit users into financial schemes
• conduct romance scams; or
• attempt to manipulate another member for financial gain.
• send or promote phishing links, fraudulent websites, or other deceptive links intended to obtain personal, financial, login, or account information.
If someone asks you for money or financial assistance, we strongly encourage you to stop communicating with them and report the account immediately.

Accounts involved in scams or financial fraud may be permanently removed and, where appropriate, reported to the relevant authorities.

 
9. RESPECT PRIVACY

Privacy helps everyone feel safer on Dater.

Respect the personal information and boundaries of other members.

You must not:

• publish another person’s private information
• share another member’s phone number without permission
• share another member’s email address without permission
• share another member’s home or workplace address
• publish private conversations without permission where prohibited by applicable law
• doxx another member
• impersonate Dater employees or customer support; or
• use personal information to threaten, blackmail, or harass another person.
 
To help protect our community, do not include your phone number, email address, or social media usernames in your profile, including services such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), or similar platforms.

Keeping conversations on Dater helps us investigate reports, respond to safety concerns, and maintain a safer community for everyone.

 
10. OFF-PLATFORM CONTACT & UNWANTED CONTACT

We respect everyone’s right to decide who they communicate with and where they communicate.

If another member indicates that they do not want further contact, you must respect that decision.

You must not:

• repeatedly contact someone after they have asked you to stop
• create another account to contact someone who has blocked or removed you
• use another person’s account to bypass a block or restriction
• search for or contact someone through another platform after they have clearly indicated that they do not want further contact
• repeatedly send friend requests, comments, or messages after being rejected
• use Dater to follow, monitor, threaten, or harass someone outside the Services; or
• encourage others to contact or harass someone on your behalf.
 
Moving a conversation to another platform is a personal choice. However, attempting to bypass another person’s boundaries, a Dater block, or an enforcement action by contacting them elsewhere is not permitted.

If someone blocks you, removes you, declines your friend request, or asks you to stop communicating, respect their decision and move on.

Conduct that occurs outside Dater may also result in action on your Dater account where it involves people you met through Dater and reasonably affects their safety, security, or well-being.

13. CONSENT & SEXUAL BOUNDARIES

Consent matters in every interaction, both online and offline.

Consent means that everyone involved freely agrees to an interaction. Consent must be voluntary, informed, and ongoing. Someone can change their mind at any time.

You must:

• respect another person’s boundaries
• accept “no” without pressure
• stop when someone asks you to stop
• respect a person’s decision not to communicate or meet
• never pressure someone into sexual activity
• never use threats, manipulation, intimidation, or coercion to obtain sexual activity; and
• respect consent during both online and offline interactions.
 
You must not:

• engage in sexual activity without consent
• pressure or manipulate someone into sexual activity
• continue sexual contact after consent has been withdrawn
• take advantage of someone who cannot provide meaningful consent
• share intimate images or videos of another person without their consent
• threaten to share intimate content
• use intimate content to blackmail or control another person; or
• encourage or facilitate non-consensual sexual activity.
 
Consent to one activity does not automatically mean consent to another activity. Consent to communicate online does not mean consent to meet in person, and agreeing to meet does not mean agreeing to sexual activity.

If someone says no, withdraws consent, blocks you, or clearly indicates that they do not want further contact, respect their decision immediately.

Violations involving sexual coercion, non-consensual sexual activity, intimate-image abuse, or sexual exploitation may result in immediate suspension or permanent removal from Dater and may be reported to appropriate authorities where required or permitted by applicable law.

 
14. VIOLENCE, GRAPHIC CONTENT & SELF-HARM

Dater should be a place where people can connect without being exposed to threats, graphic violence, or content that encourages serious harm.

• encourage another person to hurt themselves
• provide instructions intended to facilitate self-harm or suicide
• use threats of self-harm to manipulate, control, or pressure another person; or
• encourage dangerous challenges or activities that could reasonably result in serious injury or death.
 
We understand that people may discuss difficult personal experiences, mental health struggles, grief, or recovery. Sharing personal experiences or seeking support is not, by itself, a violation of these Guidelines.

However, content that encourages, glorifies, promotes, or facilitates serious harm may be removed.

Threats of violence, credible threats of serious harm, or other dangerous conduct may result in immediate enforcement action. Where appropriate and permitted or required by applicable law, Dater may preserve relevant information and cooperate with emergency services or law enforcement authorities.

24. OUR ENFORCEMENT APPROACH

We want Dater to remain a safe, respectful, and authentic community. When these Community Guidelines are violated, we may take appropriate action to protect our members and the integrity of the platform.

Depending on the nature and seriousness of a violation, Dater may:

• remove content
• require profile changes
• remove profile photos
• issue warnings
• temporarily limit certain features
• disable messaging
• restrict profile visibility
• remove verification status or badges
• temporarily suspend an account
• permanently remove an account
• prevent future account creation
• block devices or other technical identifiers used to protect the platform
• preserve relevant information where reasonably necessary; or
• cooperate with law enforcement or regulatory authorities where required or permitted by applicable law.
 
Not every violation will result in the same action. We consider factors such as the severity of the conduct, whether it is repeated, the potential risk to other members, and other relevant circumstances.

Enforcement actions may be applied individually or in combination, and Dater is not required to follow a particular sequence of warnings, restrictions, suspensions, or account removal.

Where conduct occurring outside the Services involves members, interactions, or relationships originating through Dater and reasonably affects user safety, security, privacy, or well-being, Dater may consider that conduct when making enforcement decisions.

For serious violations involving child safety, fraud, scams, threats of violence, sexual exploitation, or other harmful conduct, we may take immediate enforcement action without prior warning.

These Guidelines cannot cover every possible situation. Dater may take appropriate action against conduct that is not specifically described in these Guidelines where we reasonably believe the conduct poses a significant risk to the safety, security, privacy, or well-being of our members or the integrity of the Services.

When reviewing an appeal, we may consider:

Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that an enforcement decision will be reversed.

Submitting an appeal does not automatically restore an account, content, feature, or access that has been restricted or removed while the appeal is under review.

Repeated abusive, malicious, or frivolous appeals may be declined without further review.
